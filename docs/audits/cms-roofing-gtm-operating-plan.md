## 1. The numbers we'll run the business on

These are the numbers "imperative to know." Most will be *estimates* at Day 14 and *measured* by Day 60.

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed-to-lead** | Minutes from inquiry to first human contact | The #1 driver of inspections booked |
| **Lead → inspection %** | Inspections ÷ leads, by source | Shows which sources are real |
| **Inspection → signed %** (close rate) | Signed ÷ inspections, by source and by rep | Finds coaching gaps and bad lead sources |
| **Average ticket** | By repair / replacement / insurance / commercial | Drives every forecast |
| **Gross margin per job** | (Revenue − materials − labor − subs) ÷ revenue | CAC has to be judged against margin, not revenue |
| **CAC (cost to acquire a customer)** | (Marketing spend + sales cost for the channel) ÷ new customers from that channel | Tells us where the next dollar goes |
| **LTV (lifetime value)** | Gross margin from first job + repeat work (repairs, gutters, siding, maintenance) + margin from referrals they send | A roof is a 20+ year purchase. LTV has to come from add-ons, maintenance, and referrals. |
| **LTV : CAC** | LTV ÷ CAC | Aim for 3:1 or better per channel; cut channels below 1.5:1 |
| **Unsold-estimate pipeline** | Dollar value of estimates given but not signed | Usually the fastest money in a roofing company |
| **Backlog** | Signed dollars not yet installed | Keeps sales pace matched to crew capacity |

**Forecast model (built by Day 45):**
`Leads by source × lead→inspection % × close % × average ticket = revenue`, adjusted for season and storms. It's a live sheet, so you can ask "what if we add 20 commercial leads a month?" and see the answer.

## 2. Outbound: who we go after (ICP)

CMS's credentials (GAF Master Elite, Versico commercial, FORTIFIED, 20 years, two offices) win the **repeat, multi-roof buyers**. A single homeowner buys one roof every 20 years. A property manager or facility owner buys every year.

### Tier 1: Commercial property owners & facility managers (low-slope/flat roofs)
- **Who:** Industrial and warehouse owners, manufacturers, retail centers, churches, private schools, medical offices, auto dealerships in Warren, Daviess, Barren, Simpson, Logan counties + northern TN.
- **Titles:** Facilities Manager/Director, Plant Manager, Maintenance Manager, Operations Manager, Property Manager, Business Administrator (churches/schools), Owner/President (<50 employees).
- **Buying triggers:** Roof age 15+ years, a recent hail/wind event, active leaks, a building sale or refinance, insurance renewal or inspection.
- **Offer:** Free Versico-certified roof condition report with photos, remaining-life estimate, and a repair-vs-replace budget for next year. Then sell a **maintenance agreement** (recurring revenue) that feeds replacement jobs.
- **Why first:** Largest tickets, recurring maintenance, and fewer local competitors certified to do it.

### Tier 2: Residential property managers & investors
- **Who:** Companies managing single-family rentals and small multifamily; investors with 5+ doors (county property records show owners with multiple parcels).
- **Titles:** Owner/Broker, Property Manager, Maintenance Coordinator, Asset Manager.
- **Offer:** Priority repair response (e.g. 48-hour leak response) + portfolio roof inventory + volume pricing on replacements.

### Tier 3: HOAs & condo associations
- **Who:** Associations with shared roofs, and the management companies that run them.
- **Titles:** HOA Board President/Treasurer, Community Association Manager.
- **Offer:** Roof reserve study (how much to budget, which buildings, when), timed to their budget cycle.

### Referral partners (not buyers, but they send buyers)
- **Realtors & home inspectors:** Fast pre-listing roof certifications and repairs so deals close.
- **Insurance agents:** A trusted certified contractor for claims; FORTIFIED roofs can support better terms with some insurers *(verify locally)*.
- **Storm-adjacent trades:** Gutter, siding, and restoration companies without roofing crews.

### Where the lists come from
- County property records (PVA) for owner, property type, square footage, year built
- Apollo / Clay / LinkedIn Sales Navigator for titles and contacts
- Hail/wind maps after every storm (e.g. HailTrace or Interactive Hail Maps) → target the affected zip codes and commercial buildings
- Chamber of Commerce, BBB directories, local property-management associations
- **Our own past customers and old estimates** (the list we already own)

### Outbound motion
- A 4–6 touch sequence per contact: email, call, LinkedIn, and a mailed or dropped-off condition report for Tier 1.
- Target volume once running: [X] new commercial conversations a month. Set after Day 14, based on estimator capacity.
- Measured by: meetings booked → inspections → proposals → signed, and CAC per signed account.

## 3. Inbound: capture and convert the demand we already get

**Fix the leaks first (Days 1–30)**
- **Speed-to-lead:** Instant text-back on web forms and missed calls; form auto-reply on; after-hours and weekend coverage (answering service or on-call rotation).
- **Consistent listings:** One name, address, and phone everywhere (the audit found two of each). Claim every profile.
- **Reviews engine:** An automatic review request after every completed job; reply to every review, starting with the negative ones.
- **Better form:** Address, service type, insurance claim yes/no → routed to the right person.

**Then grow it (Days 30–90)**
- **Google Local Services Ads** (the "Google Guaranteed" listings above the Maps results; pay per lead). Test in Bowling Green first.
- **Local SEO:** City pages for Elizabethtown, Franklin, Glasgow, Portland TN, Russellville, and Smiths Grove, plus a storm-response page that goes live after every event.
- **Financing** on every quote and on the website.
- **Storm-response playbook:** When hail hits, same day: landing page, Meta/Google ads geofenced to affected zips, text/email to past customers in the area, door-hanger routes.

**The biggest hidden money: follow-up**
- **Unsold estimates:** A 60-day follow-up sequence (text, call, email, financing option) on every quote that doesn't sign.
- **Past customers (10,000+ roofs):** Yearly check-up offer, post-storm inspection, gutters/siding cross-sell, referral bonus.

## 5. Training & SOPs

Written, short, and trained in person. Each one gets a checklist and a script:

1. **Lead intake & speed-to-lead:** answering, qualifying, booking the inspection
2. **Inspection & presentation:** what the estimator shows, financing and warranty talk track, asking for the signature
3. **Follow-up:** unsigned estimates, cadence, when to let go
4. **Insurance claim process:** for the customer and for the office
5. **Job-close:** photo/video capture, review request, referral ask
6. **Commercial outbound:** research, sequence, condition-report delivery
7. **Weekly numbers meeting:** 30 minutes, same dashboard every week
